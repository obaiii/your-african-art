About Your African Art

Making authentic African art accessible to homes worldwide.

The Mission

Art that belongs in every home.

African art has always been extraordinary — vibrant, expressive, deeply human. But for too long, accessing it has been difficult. High prices, poor shipping, inconsistent presentation. We're here to change that.

Your African Art is a curated platform connecting talented African artists with art lovers around the world. We hand-select original paintings, present them beautifully, and deliver them globally — all at accessible prices.

Whether you're in Lagos, London, Atlanta, or Toronto, we believe you should be able to put something real, something meaningful, on your walls without the gallery markup.

What We Stand For

Our values.
