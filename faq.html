Ordering & Products

Each painting is carefully rolled (paint side out) and shipped inside a custom-cut 2-inch PVC tube. PVC tubes are waterproof, crush-resistant, and protect the canvas throughout international transit. The tube length is cut to match your specific painting — see our Delivery & Care page for the full tube size reference and unpacking instructions.

Shipping & Delivery

We ship worldwide. Our primary markets are the UK, US, Canada, and across Europe, but we deliver anywhere with a postal address.

International delivery typically takes 7–14 business days depending on your location. Lagos and Abuja delivery is arranged locally after payment. You'll receive tracking or delivery coordination details once your order ships.

International shipping is calculated by delivery country, tube size, and DHL-style chargeable weight. Each painting is rolled into a 2-inch PVC tube; the checkout estimates the tube package from the painting dimensions, handling margin, and shipment weight, then shows the total in your selected country's currency. PayPal will show the final conversion before you pay. Local delivery within Lagos or Abuja is NGN 10,000. Other delivery locations can be quoted before payment.

Caring for Your Art

Keep your painting away from direct sunlight and high humidity. Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid touching the painted surface directly. If stretched and framed, hang on a secure wall hook away from heat sources and moisture.

Take your canvas to any local framing shop to have it stretched onto wooden stretcher bars (typically $20–$60). You can also do it yourself — we have a full step-by-step DIY stretching guide on our Delivery & Care page, covering gallery wraps, pin mounting, and other creative display options.

Remove the end caps from the PVC tube carefully (avoid using sharp blades near the opening). Slide the canvas out gently and lay it flat, paint side up, on a clean surface. Place soft weights on the corners and let it relax for 24–48 hours. Any minor curl will flatten naturally. Never iron or apply direct heat to the painted surface. For detailed step-by-step instructions, see our Delivery & Care page.
